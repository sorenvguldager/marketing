FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is SMS marketing?

SMS marketing is a communication method where businesses send text messages to customers and potential customers. It's used to share offers, news, reminders, and personal messages directly to the recipient's mobile phone.

Why use SMS marketing?

SMS marketing has an open rate of up to 98% — far higher than email. Messages are delivered in seconds and typically read within minutes, making it the most effective channel for time-sensitive messages and offers.

How much does SMS marketing cost?

The price depends on the number of messages and your agreement. inMobile offers flexible pricing plans that suit both small and large businesses. Start with a free 14-day trial to test the platform.

How do I collect SMS consents?

You can collect consents via sign-up forms on your website, at checkout in your webshop, through competitions, pop-ups, or in physical stores. It's important to always comply with GDPR and marketing regulations.

Can I integrate with my existing webshop?

Yes! inMobile integrates with Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce, Klaviyo, Zapier, and many more. You can easily sync customer data and set up automatic flows based on customer behaviour.

Is SMS marketing effective for e-commerce?

Absolutely. SMS marketing drives direct traffic to your webshop with an average click rate of 36%. It's ideal for abandoned cart flows, flash sales, new product launches, and exclusive VIP offers.
